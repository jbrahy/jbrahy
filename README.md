# Pocket Aces

**Fractional executives, extended by AI, accountable by name.**

I'm John Brahy, founder of [Pocket Aces LLC](https://pocketacesllc.com) in Long Beach, CA.

Pocket Aces puts a senior executive in your pocket for a share of the week. Each ace leads the work, makes the decisions and answers for the result. AI agents handle the volume work under the ace's direction, and the ace reviews everything that ships. You get senior judgment and a small team's output without a full-time hire.

## Pick the role you need

| Ace | What they do |
|---|---|
| CTO | Architecture, engineering leadership and delivery |
| CAIO | AI strategy, agent engineering and governance |
| Forward Deployed Engineer | An engineer embedded in your team |
| CISO and security | Security leadership and review |
| COO and operations | Process, people and execution |
| CMO and growth | Marketing and growth |
| Chief of Sales | Pipeline and revenue |
| Quality assurance, IT, compliance and contracts | Supporting roles |

## How it works

1. **We match you with an ace.** Tell us the role and the hours, and we name the ace who fits.
2. **The ace leads.** Your ace sets priorities, makes the decisions and is accountable for the result.
3. **Agents extend the ace.** AI agents do the volume work under the ace's direction.
4. **You get a weekly report.** A clear account of what shipped and what's next.

Engagements run at 20, 10 or 5 hours a week.
